The Residency Difference

Why a residency,
not another course?

A residency is a structured, supervised clinical training program completed after formal education — where you apply real knowledge in real-world settings, under expert mentorship.

Most programs teach you theory. The HCI residency model is designed to bridge the gap between what you know and what it takes to build a successful, confident holistic health practice.

A Typical Course

Teaches you what to know. Content, theory, and concepts — but no supervised application.

HCI Residency

Teaches you how to practice. Supervised case work, live mentoring, and real-world systems.

"School teaches you what to know. A residency teaches you how to practice."
