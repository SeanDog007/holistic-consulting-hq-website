01
LAUNCH
Your Career
For New Graduates & Health Coaches

From certified to client-ready — with confidence.

You finished your program. You have the knowledge. But the business side — finding clients, setting up systems, defining your scope — feels overwhelming. LAUNCH closes that gap.

This residency gives you everything your certification didn't: real practice structure, client intake systems, business fundamentals, and the mentorship to take your first steps without second-guessing yourself.

What's Included

  • Business structure & legal setup for your practice
  • Scope of practice clarity — what you can and cannot do
  • Client intake systems, forms, and intake workflows
  • Strategies for finding and signing first paying clients
  • Imposter syndrome work & professional confidence building
  • Pricing, packaging, and how to talk about what you do
  • Access to HCI practitioner community & peer network
By the End of LAUNCH, You Will Have
  • A complete, professional client intake system
  • A clear, confident answer to "What do you do?"
  • Your first paying client — or a concrete plan to get one
  • Business structure and scope clarity sorted
  • A professional network of fellow practitioners
Good to Know

LAUNCH graduates are eligible to progress directly into the GROW residency — continuing their journey with a stronger foundation and a proven track record.

03
MASTER
Your Craft
For Confident, Established Practitioners

Specialize. Differentiate. Lead your field.

You've built your practice. Now it's time to go deeper. The MASTER residency is for practitioners who are ready to specialize in clinical herbalism and functional testing — and position themselves as recognized experts in integrative health.

Publish case studies. Work with field experts. Earn a reputation that makes you the first call when practitioners need a specialist.

What's Included

  • Clinical Herbalism full program curriculum
  • Functional testing training & interpretation
  • Live sessions with clinical herbal field experts
  • Field expert positioning & authority-building strategy
  • Case study development & publication support
  • Advanced community network & referral relationships
By the End of MASTER, You Will Have
  • Clinical herbalism expertise & credentials
  • Functional testing proficiency in practice
  • Published case studies in your specialty
  • Expert positioning in your niche field
  • A referral network of integrative practitioners
