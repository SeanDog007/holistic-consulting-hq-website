Gradient Recipes

Hero Gradient

Three layered radial gradients: emerald at 20% 50% (0.08 opacity), gold at 80% 20% (0.06), emerald at 50% 100% (0.04). Applied over warm gray base.

CTA Dark Gradient

Gold glow at 20% 80% (0.12 opacity) and emerald glow at 80% 20% (0.15 opacity) over charcoal base. Creates depth on dark sections.

05

Cards & Surfaces

Surfaces use a three-tier depth system. Each level has distinct shadow treatment and border conventions.

Depth Levels

Base

Level 1

Flat surface with subtle border and minimal shadow. For stat cards and info blocks.

Shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)
Border: 1px solid #DFE6E9
Radius: 0.5rem (rounded-lg)

Elevated

Level 2

Color-accented top border with hover lift. For program cards and featured content.

Shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)
Border-top: 3px solid accent color
Hover: translateY(-4px) + deeper shadow

Floating

Level 3

Prominent shadow for overlaid content. For floating badges, tooltips, and modals.

Shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)
Border: 1px solid #DFE6E9
Usage: Floating elements, popups

08

Animation & Motion

Only animate transform and opacity. Never use transition-all.

Scroll Reveal (Fade Up)

Animation: opacity 0→1, translateY(24px)→0

Duration: 0.7–0.8s

Easing: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

Stagger: 0.1s increments per child

Trigger: IntersectionObserver at threshold 0.15

Spring Interaction

Hover lift: translateY(-2px) buttons, translateY(-4px) cards

Duration: 0.3s

Easing: cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)

Active: translateY(0) spring-back

Applies to: Buttons, cards, nav links

Easing Curves

Reveal Decelerate

cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

Scroll-in animations

Spring Overshoot

cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)

Hover / active interactions

Ease (Standard)

ease

Color transitions only

Animation Rules

  • Only animate transform and opacity for performance
  • Never use transition-all — always specify individual properties
  • Use will-change: transform sparingly and only on elements that animate
  • Stagger child elements by 0.1s increments (delay-100, delay-200, etc.)
