# Holistic Consulting Brand — Fleet One-Pager

**Source:** Feb 2026 Brand Identity (web), Print & Collateral, Herbal Medicine sub-brand guides  
**Canonical HTML:** `guides/` · **Text extracts:** `extracts/`  
**Rule:** Load this before any HC visual or public copy ships. Full guides win on conflict.

## Voice
- Professional yet accessible. Authoritative without being academic. Warm and results-focused.
- Themes: structured clinical training; bridging knowledge → practice; practitioner career pathways.
- Key phrases: “Practice, not just certified.” · “Where Practitioners Become Professionals.” · “Impactful Careers.”

## Core palette (web / primary brand)
| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Primary / trust | Emerald | `#2E7D32` |
| CTA / prestige | Gold | `#C9963B` |
| Primary text / dark bg | Charcoal | `#2D3436` |
| Page background | Warm Gray | `#F5F0EB` |
| Hover emerald | Emerald Light | `#4CAF50` |
| Soft fill | Emerald Sage | `#E8F5E9` |
| Dark emerald | Emerald Forest | `#1B5E20` |
| Secondary text | Slate | `#636E72` |
| Borders | Border Gray | `#DFE6E9` |

**Never** default Tailwind blues/indigos. Body text charcoal/slate — not pure `#000000`.

## Typography
- **Display:** Cormorant Garamond (serif) — headlines, hero, section titles. Never for body.
- **Body / UI:** DM Sans — body, labels, nav, buttons.
- Print minimums: body ≥9pt (10pt preferred); captions ≥7pt; embed fonts in PDFs.

## Buttons (light bg)
- Primary CTA: Gold `#C9963B` → hover `#D4A84B` + slight lift
- Secondary: Emerald outline `#2E7D32` → fill on hover
- Animate only `transform` and `opacity` (no `transition-all`)

## Herbal Medicine sub-brand (when herbal content)
Sage `#6B7F5E` · Copper `#B07242` · Herbchar `#2A3024` · Parchment `#F5F1E6`  
Voice: warm, knowledgeable, grounding; “Rooted in tradition, guided by science.”

## Thumbnails / social
- Prefer Brand Templates + real stills; no Canva Magic Design finals; no AI faces as finals.
- See also thumbnail brand-kit when Thumbnail bot is unpaused.

## Do / Don’t (fleet)
**Do:** Emerald + gold on warm gray; Cormorant + DM Sans pair; gold for primary CTAs; herbal palette only for herbal lane.  
**Don’t:** Invent a parallel palette; mix herbal copper into core career marketing; ship AI-slop thumbnails; skip this kit for “just a quick graphic.”
